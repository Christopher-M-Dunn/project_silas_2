# Spiral Token

*Not yet named. Found Day 3, dawn, on [The Terraced Shore](../locations/the-terraced-shore.md).*

A small, dense, dark ovoid — stone or something stone-like, barely bigger than a chicken egg but heavy as metal — worked into a smooth deliberate shape with a single fine spiral line etched around its length, the groove depth varying slightly in a way that reads as hand-worked rather than machined. Warm to the touch, though it sat in cliff-shadow with no sun on it, and the warmth isn't constant: a faint, slow rise and fall against the palm, roughly a thirtieth of a human pulse. No seams, no markings besides the spiral, no obvious way to open or activate it.

Found tucked into a made niche, square-cut into the rock, at exactly hand height on the third terrace up from the water — placed, not dropped or hidden, precisely where a searching hand would find it. Later, its maker turned out to be [The Terrace Dweller](../characters/the-terrace-dweller.md), glimpsed just past the doorway the token led toward.

**Where:** carried by the player.
