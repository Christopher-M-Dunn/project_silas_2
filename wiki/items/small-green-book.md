# Small Green Book

Recovered from a chest of [[maren-ashwood]]'s late father's belongings, in the loft above the [[sleeping-dragon-tavern]]. Small enough to wrap in a shirt, bound now in a length of Maren's own red hair-cord.

[[silas-ashwood|Silas]] believes something evil, centered on this book, is sapping his strength and clouding his mind. When [[player|Christopher]] unwrapped it, the book fought back — see [[binding-of-the-green-book]] — and now sits contained inside a stable bluish-purple glow of Christopher's making, worn around his neck on Maren's red hair-cord.

By Christopher's assessment: a magical familiar, female-presenting by whatever sense he uses to know such things, at a strength unlike anything he's encountered. She's spoken to Silas continuously for weeks — quieting, but not stopping, two or three days after the binding, then returning three days ago in a way Silas describes as patient rather than raging, as if she'd settled on a strategy. Christopher now suspects the binding may not have truly caught her at all — that she could be "playing dead" while she works out what he's worth to her. He means to unbind the book to study her properly, and both he and Silas agree they need to seek out "the elders" — some authority of Christopher's, not yet described — before going further.
