# Small Green Book

Recovered from a chest of [[maren-ashwood]]'s late father's belongings, in the loft above the [[sleeping-dragon-tavern]]. Small enough to wrap in a shirt, bound now in a length of Maren's own red hair-cord.

[[silas-ashwood|Silas]] believes something evil, centered on this book, is sapping his strength and clouding his mind. When [[player|Christopher]] unwrapped it, the book fought back — see [[binding-of-the-green-book]] — and now sits contained inside a stable bluish-purple glow of Christopher's making. Whether that containment is truly safe, and what the book actually is or does, is still unknown. Christopher means to carry it to his laboratory in RavenCraw to find out.
