# The Realm of Aldenmere

A medieval fantasy world of ancient forests, crumbling kingdoms, and forgotten magic. The present corner of it is the town of **Briarhollow** and the **Thornwood Forest** at its eastern edge.

## Briarhollow

A small timber-and-cobblestone town. Its heart is the [[briarhollow-town-square]] with its weathered fountain; around it stand the [[sleeping-dragon-tavern]] (a minute's walk from the square), the [[blacksmiths-forge]] to the east, and the [[old-chapel]] to the north. The [[briarhollow-east-gate]], a couple of minutes southeast of the square, is the only way out toward the forest.

Lately someone has been stealing from the market stalls at night — the talk of the square.

## The Thornwood

Beyond the east gate, a five-minute walk down a dirt road, the ancient oaks of the [[thornwood-forest-edge]] close overhead. Ten minutes deeper lies the [[thornwood-clearing]], where sunlight breaks through onto mossy stones and an old stone well with a frayed rope. The forest is not tame; sensible townsfolk go in pairs and come out before dark.

## People

[[maren-ashwood]] keeps the tavern. [[kael-ironhand]] works the forge. [[sister-lenara]] tends the chapel. [[finn-quickfoot]] lodges at the tavern, selling this and that. And a [[player|traveler]] has lately been sleeping by the tavern hearth.
