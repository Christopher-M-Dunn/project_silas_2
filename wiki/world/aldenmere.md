# The Realm of Aldenmere

A medieval fantasy world of ancient forests, crumbling kingdoms, and forgotten magic. The present corner of it is the town of **Briarhollow** and the **Thornwood Forest** at its eastern edge.

## Briarhollow

A small timber-and-cobblestone town. Its heart is the [Briarhollow Town Square](../locations/briarhollow-town-square.md) with its weathered fountain; around it stand the [The Sleeping Dragon Tavern](../locations/sleeping-dragon-tavern.md) (a minute's walk from the square), the [The Blacksmith's Forge](../locations/blacksmiths-forge.md) to the east, and the [The Old Chapel](../locations/old-chapel.md) to the north. The [Briarhollow East Gate](../locations/briarhollow-east-gate.md), a couple of minutes southeast of the square, is the only way out toward the forest.

Lately someone has been stealing from the market stalls at night — the talk of the square.

## The Thornwood

Beyond the east gate, a five-minute walk down a dirt road, the ancient oaks of the [Thornwood Forest Edge](../locations/thornwood-forest-edge.md) close overhead. Ten minutes deeper lies the [Thornwood Clearing](../locations/thornwood-clearing.md), where sunlight breaks through onto mossy stones and an old stone well with a frayed rope. The forest is not tame; sensible townsfolk go in pairs and come out before dark.

## The north road

A road runs north out of Briarhollow toward farther towns. Several days' hard travel along it lies [RavenCraw](../locations/ravencraw.md), a small walled town of which little has yet been seen.

## People

[Maren Ashwood](../characters/maren-ashwood.md) keeps the tavern — though she has lately turned out her regulars and barred its doors. [Kael Ironhand](../characters/kael-ironhand.md) works the forge. [Sister Lenara](../characters/sister-lenara.md) tends the chapel. [Finn Quickfoot](../characters/finn-quickfoot.md) lodges at the tavern — or did, until it closed. And the strange [traveler](../characters/player.md) who slept by the Dragon's hearth woke one morning and left town.
