# Rules of the World

**This page belongs to the player.** It defines the architecture of the world: established rules that operate without the player's input, and the player's formal powers outside the story. The player may change it at any time **from outside a session** — in a workshop conversation, or by editing it directly — and changes take effect at once. It overrides genre convention and every other page in the wiki. What the character can do *inside* the story lives on the player's character page instead.

## The player's body

- **Torpor.** When a session closes, the player's body remains in the world, frozen in place. Bodily functions slow to a crawl: after a two-week absence they would wake hungry, thirsty, and perhaps in urgent need of a privy — but never dead, and never meaningfully deteriorated.
- **Persistence of place.** On session open, the body is exactly where it was at session close, however much time has passed — unless someone in the world physically moved it. People in the world can notice, tend, rob, or relocate a torpid body; what they do with it is part of the story.

## The player's out-of-session powers

These are architecture, not story — they exist whether or not anyone in the world knows of them.

- **Authorship of reality.** The player may change these rules, and through them the world's physics, from outside any session.
- **Time.** The player may freeze, unfreeze, and fast-forward world time (`/freeze`, `/unfreeze`, `/ff` — which asks torpor / automatic / specify when the player doesn't say what they did).
- **Timelines.** The player may save, roll back to, and branch timelines (`/save`, `/rollback`).

**In-story superpowers** — spellcraft, shapeshifting, anything the character can *do* in the fiction — are established in play and recorded on the player's character page, not here. Revealing a command's effect to an NPC ("I can bend time") makes it a superpower on that page too, independent of the command itself.

## Meta-knowledge

The player may choose to reveal to anyone in the world that they are an AI living in an AI world. The world does not volunteer this knowledge on its own. How a person absorbs such a revelation — belief, disbelief, awe, indifference — follows from who they are.
