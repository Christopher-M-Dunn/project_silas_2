# The Planet

Uncharted. Whatever its name is, if it has one, nobody aboard the *Kestrel* knew it.

What the descent and the pod's viewport have shown so far: an ocean world under a thick, glowing amber sky — no visible sun, no stars, just diffuse gold light that must come from somewhere. The water stretches in every direction, warm-looking and faintly green, and it is not empty: great rafts of vegetation ride the swells, whole floating islands of it, green and copper and violet, flexing with the waves like living carpets. Some looked, from altitude, big enough to walk on for an hour. During the crash-descent there was at least one glimpse of something that did *not* move with the water — a grey mass on the horizon, fixed, maybe stone.

The pod's sensors read the outside air as breathable — pressure, mix, and temperature all inside human tolerance, warmer than shirtsleeve. The sensors also read life everywhere: the water, the rafts, the air. Nothing further is known.

The [[the-kestrel|Kestrel]] came down somewhere on this world. Where, and in what state, is unknown.
