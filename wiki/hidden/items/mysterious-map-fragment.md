# Mysterious Map Fragment — HIDDEN

*Undiscovered by the player; publicly, this item does not exist.*

A torn piece of parchment with faded ink markings, showing part of a route into the Thornwood. Read rightly, the route leads to the [Thornwood Clearing](../../locations/thornwood-clearing.md) — and marks the old stone well itself, with a symbol matching the wards in the [The Old Chapel](../../locations/old-chapel.md)'s crypt (see [Sister Lenara](../../characters/sister-lenara.md)'s hidden page).

**Where it actually is:** sewn into the lining of [Finn Quickfoot](../../characters/finn-quickfoot.md)'s coat. He lifted it from a drunk courier in a river-town six weeks ago; the courier's employer is the matter the bounty hunter Vess wants settled (see Finn's hidden page).
