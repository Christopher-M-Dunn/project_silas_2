# Mysterious Map Fragment — HIDDEN

*Undiscovered by the player; publicly, this item does not exist.*

A torn piece of parchment with faded ink markings, showing part of a route into the Thornwood. Read rightly, the route leads to the [[thornwood-clearing]] — and marks the old stone well itself, with a symbol matching the wards in the [[old-chapel]]'s crypt (see [[sister-lenara]]'s hidden page).

**Where it actually is:** sewn into the lining of [[finn-quickfoot]]'s coat. He lifted it from a drunk courier in a river-town six weeks ago; the courier's employer is the matter the bounty hunter Vess wants settled (see Finn's hidden page).
