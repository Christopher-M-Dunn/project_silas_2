# The Spiral Token — HIDDEN

*Unknown to the player: Veth-made, deliberately placed as a first overture. Draft, subject to retcon until narrated.*

A dense, dark ovoid worked by Veth hands — not grown, not natural, a genuine tool of their craft though its purpose is closer to a greeting card than a tool: warmth held in the material on purpose, by whatever means Veth work stone, so that a stranger's hand would register *made for you to hold* rather than *found lying around*. Placed in a made niche at exactly hand height on the third terrace, timed to be found by a searching newcomer rather than handed over — the same instinct that had the survey leave something for [[roland-voss]]: test the reaction to a small, safe overture before risking a face.

Whoever placed it is local to this terrace, not the wreck survey — a different Veth, or few, tending this garden shore, who noticed the newcomer's arrival overnight (the growth-sounds of [[first-ktar|Rusty]] working, or the newcomer's own movement at dawn) and chose to leave this rather than show themselves yet. They are almost certainly still near, watching from within the rock, waiting to see what's done with it.

The slow pulse in its warmth is deliberate too — not a malfunction or a side effect, a heartbeat-analog worked in on purpose, the closest thing to "this was made by something alive, for something alive" the Veth had a way to say without shared language yet.

**Currently:** in the player's hand, warm, spiral-etched. Origin now strongly implied — [[the-terrace-dweller]] is almost certainly its maker — though never stated outright.
