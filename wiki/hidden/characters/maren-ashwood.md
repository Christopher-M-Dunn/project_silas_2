# Maren Ashwood — HIDDEN

## True feelings

- **[[kael-ironhand]]** — trusts him like family, and knows more than she lets on: her gossip network long ago told her a Stonefall man came asking questions about a runaway smith, two towns over, three years back. She never told Kael. She's decided that if trouble comes for him, it will have to come through her taproom first.
- **[[finn-quickfoot]]** — is certain, not merely suspicious, that Finn is behind the market-stall thefts; she's twice found stall-goods among his "wares." She hasn't confronted him because she can't yet prove it, and because — infuriatingly — she likes him.
- **[[sister-lenara]]** — the one person in Briarhollow Maren is slightly afraid of, though she'd never say so. Two centuries of anything isn't natural.
- **[[player|Christopher]]**, the traveler by her hearth: his unwakeable sleep unsettled her, and she'd taken to draping a blanket over him at closing and telling herself it was for the look of the place. He's woken, greeted her by name — a name she never gave him — and named himself a practitioner of magic "both light and dark," with a laboratory in RavenCraw. When she asked him to explain himself on the walk to the loft, he said nothing at all — she noticed, and it went into the ledger she's keeping on him. Then she watched him fight something out of her father's book with his bare voice, nearly bring the loft down doing it, and calmly inform her he'd just rung a dinner bell for wraiths across a hundred miles. She's frightened of him now in a way she wasn't an hour ago, and frightened *for* him too, in a way that confuses her — gratitude and dread tangled together, with no time to sort out which is winning before she has to decide whether to follow him out the door.
- **Silas Ashwood**, her brother: eleven years of silence she'd made peace with by assuming he was dead — it hurt less than the alternative. Learning he's alive and in RavenCraw reopened something she thought scarred over; learning in the same breath that he's "not well," possessed by something evil centered on the book, has knocked the anger clean out of her and left only fear. Finding him alive at the end of her own long walk north — thin, wrecked, but breathing, and saying her name like it's the first solid thing he's held onto in months — is the first unqualified relief she's felt since Christopher said "Silas" in her taproom eleven days ago. She hasn't had a second yet to process what she walked in on with Christopher.

## Secrets

- Her late husband didn't die of fever, as the town believes; he drank himself into the river. She keeps the fever story for his dignity and her peace.
- Through her gossip network she has heard that a scarred bounty hunter — a woman called **Vess** — has been working the river-towns asking after *a halfling thief*. Maren has not yet connected this to Finn out loud, even to herself.
- There's a wooden chest of her late father's belongings in the loft above the tavern's taproom, mostly gone through now for the first time in nine years — the rest of her parents' things went to the poor box the week they were buried.

## Tasks

- **Done:** got everyone out of the Sleeping Dragon and locked it up ahead of the wraith threat — no wraith ever came.
- **Done:** reach Silas in RavenCraw. Left Briarhollow the 16th, eleven hard days on foot for a woman who hadn't traveled past the market in a decade, asked her way to the Slippery Gypsy Inn on arrival, and walked in on Karl about to force the door — timed, as far as she knows, to nothing but luck.
- **Active:** get real answers — from Silas about what's actually wrong with him, and from Christopher about what she just walked in on and who, exactly, he really is.
- **Interrupted:** catch the market thief with proof, before the stall-keepers start blaming each other. — interrupted by the wraith threat and the tavern evacuation.
- **Planned:** quietly warn Kael if the Stonefall questions ever come closer than two towns away.
- **Ongoing:** keep the tavern running and profitable; it is fifteen years of her life and all of his that's left.
