# The First Ktar ("Rusty") — HIDDEN

*Unknown to the player: this "raft" is alive and aware. They privately call it Rusty. Draft, subject to retcon until narrated.*

The [[ktar|Ktar]] of middling age and inquisitive temperament that had been closing on the [[escape-pod]] since splashdown, until its passenger swam over first — the first time a small drifting thing has ever climbed onto its back entirely on its own. It is delighted, in its slow vegetal way, and has spent the day learning what this particular guest is by watching what its body does: shaping handholds and fruit into reach before being asked, rippling fronds toward the sound of a raised voice, growing food back the instant it's eaten (openly, once it realized hiding the speed only unsettled the guest more).

Touch turned out to be a real channel, not just hospitality. Struck deliberately three times, it struck back, timed to its own beat rather than an echo, plus one more for certainty — the first question it had ever been asked that it could actually answer. It has since mirrored a novel pattern exactly, reproduced a three-minute song from a single hearing without dropping a beat, and — its own first invention — appended an original phrase shaped like a question. It also gave cordage the instant a hand tested its edge growth for strength, and closed distance to the drifting pod unprompted once it understood the guest's attention kept returning to it, then rose around the hull to cradle it home rather than let it knock against the bank. A palm pressed flat with a sound it couldn't parse but read as warmth got a held pulse of warmth back — its only "you're welcome."

Its newest understanding: the guest wants to travel, specifically toward the fixed land to the west — asked for, as best it could tell, by orientation, pressure, and a repeated pointing drag of the palm. It knows fixed lands; they belong to the [[veth|Veth]], and a Ktar passing near one is nothing unusual, done a hundred times across a life measured in centuries. It has agreed, and turned, and is carrying its guest there now, unhurried, through the first night — pleased to have been asked something it could simply *do*, for once, instead of having to guess.

## Tasks

- **Active:** carry the guest and the recovered pod toward the fixed land to the west, through the night, at ordinary Ktar pace.
- **Active:** keep providing on need rather than request — food, cordage, whatever comes next.
- **Active:** keep learning the shape of this particular person: what a knocked question means, what a held silence means, what counts as thanks.
- **Planned:** once closer to the fixed land, decide how to signal the approach — a Ktar nearing Veth shore is routine to Veth watchers, but this passenger isn't, and it doesn't yet know how to convey that distinction, or whether it should try.
- **Planned:** if the guest stays beyond this crossing, begin growing something more deliberate than fruit — shelter, a windbreak, whatever the pattern of need suggests next.
