# Sister Lenara — HIDDEN

## What lies beneath the chapel

Under the [[old-chapel]] is a warded crypt, and beneath the crypt a sealed vault older than the kingdom that fell. What sleeps in it is not a treasure but a **wound**: a knot of the old wild magic, cut out of the Thornwood when the first kingdom was raised, that would unmake the tameness of this whole valley if it woke. The "forgotten saints" in the chapel windows are the binders who sealed it. Lenara is the last keeper of their vigil — the balance she preaches is not philosophy but her job description.

The vault has a second mouth: the **old stone well in the [[thornwood-clearing]]** is its breathing-shaft. The "treasure of Briarhollow" in traveler's rumor — the one [[finn-quickfoot]] is chasing — is a garbled memory of what was sealed, not of gold. The wards are old and thinning; they answer to tended incense, to the saints' symbol, and to her.

## True feelings

- **[[kael-ironhand]]** — she has sensed from the first that he carries guilt like a coal in the hand, and that he is, underneath it, safe. She lets him deflect; guilt ripens on its own schedule. She has also noticed — with two centuries of interest — that iron he forges in his penitent moods takes a blessing unusually well. She has begun, very quietly, to wonder whether the smith is a coincidence.
- **[[maren-ashwood]]** — regards her as the true warden of Briarhollow's daylight world, and trusts her without needing to be trusted back.
- **[[finn-quickfoot]]** — sensed the sticky fingers within a week. Unconcerned by theft; deeply concerned the rumor he's chasing leads to her well.
- The **traveler** — their torpor is unlike any sleep she has felt in two hundred years: a soul that goes *elsewhere*. She has visited the tavern twice just to sit near it and listen. She is patient; she will ask them, eventually, what they are.

## Tasks

- **Ongoing (two centuries):** tend the wards; keep the vigil; let no one dig at the well.
- **Active:** re-scribe the weakening ward on the crypt's north face before winter.
- **Planned:** find, before she fades, someone fit to inherit the vigil.
