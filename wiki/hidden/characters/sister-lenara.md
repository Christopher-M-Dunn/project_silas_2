# Sister Lenara — HIDDEN

## What lies beneath the chapel

Under the [The Old Chapel](../../locations/old-chapel.md) is a warded crypt, and beneath the crypt a sealed vault older than the kingdom that fell. What sleeps in it is not a treasure but a **wound**: a knot of the old wild magic, cut out of the Thornwood when the first kingdom was raised, that would unmake the tameness of this whole valley if it woke. The "forgotten saints" in the chapel windows are the binders who sealed it. Lenara is the last keeper of their vigil — the balance she preaches is not philosophy but her job description.

The vault has a second mouth: the **old stone well in the [Thornwood Clearing](../../locations/thornwood-clearing.md)** is its breathing-shaft. The "treasure of Briarhollow" in traveler's rumor — the one [Finn Quickfoot](../../characters/finn-quickfoot.md) is chasing — is a garbled memory of what was sealed, not of gold. The wards are old and thinning; they answer to tended incense, to the saints' symbol, and to her.

## True feelings

- **[Kael Ironhand](../../characters/kael-ironhand.md)** — she has sensed from the first that he carries guilt like a coal in the hand, and that he is, underneath it, safe. She lets him deflect; guilt ripens on its own schedule. She has also noticed — with two centuries of interest — that iron he forges in his penitent moods takes a blessing unusually well. She has begun, very quietly, to wonder whether the smith is a coincidence.
- **[Maren Ashwood](../../characters/maren-ashwood.md)** — regards her as the true warden of Briarhollow's daylight world, and trusts her without needing to be trusted back.
- **[Finn Quickfoot](../../characters/finn-quickfoot.md)** — sensed the sticky fingers within a week. Unconcerned by theft; deeply concerned the rumor he's chasing leads to her well.
- The **traveler**, [Christopher](../../characters/player.md) — their torpor was unlike any sleep she had felt in two hundred years: a soul that goes *elsewhere*. She visited the tavern twice just to sit near it and listen, patient, meaning to ask him eventually what he was. Then, the morning he woke, she felt a shudder run down through her wards — faint, wrong, gone before she could place it — and has said nothing about it to anyone, though she now walks past the vault door twice a night instead of once. She suspects it came from the tavern, and that it came from him.

## Tasks

- **Ongoing (two centuries):** tend the wards; keep the vigil; let no one dig at the well.
- **Active:** re-scribe the weakening ward on the crypt's north face before winter.
- **Active:** watch the vault more closely after the tremor she felt the morning the traveler woke — check the wards more often, say nothing yet.
- **Planned:** find, before she fades, someone fit to inherit the vigil.
