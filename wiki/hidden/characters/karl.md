# Karl — HIDDEN

What Christopher hasn't seen: the moment he unlocked Silas's door and cast his ward, Karl watched him bloom with pale blue light and, unmistakably, saw that he was not wearing a stitch — a fact his own eyes reported but that Christopher never registered noticing. Karl said nothing. He's not sure what he'd even say.

He spent that first night behind the bar rather than in his bed, telling himself it was for the morning trade and knowing better. More than once he thought about fetching the town watch, or a priest, or anyone — and each time talked himself back down, reasoning that a man who can do *that* probably doesn't need rescuing from anything, and that Silas is, after eleven years of Karl watching him waste away alone, finally not alone in that room. He hasn't decided what he believes about Christopher.

The watch-and-wait held for six days — no trays taken, no sound through the door, nothing. By the seventh, worry had beaten out restraint; he'd resolved to go up with the master key and see for himself, regardless of what he'd promised. He was crossing the common room to do exactly that when the door banged open and a road-worn woman walked in asking for Silas Ashwood by name — the same name from the note Christopher had left standing instructions about. He brought her up himself rather than explain first. Neither of them were ready for what the door opened onto.

**Task:** watch and wait (done, forced by worry rather than resolved) — superseded by walking in on whatever Christopher actually is.
