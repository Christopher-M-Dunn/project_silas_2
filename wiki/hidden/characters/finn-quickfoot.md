# Finn Quickfoot — HIDDEN

## The real trade

Finn is the market thief — [Maren Ashwood](../../characters/maren-ashwood.md)'s instinct is exactly right. He lifts from the stalls at night and re-sells the goods as "traveling stock," mostly to people from the next town over. He'd call it redistribution with a finder's fee. He steals nothing from the tavern: partly professional courtesy, mostly fear of Maren's eyes.

## The map and the hunter

Sewn into his coat lining is a [Mysterious Map Fragment](../items/mysterious-map-fragment.md) he lifted six weeks ago from a drunk courier in a river-town — before he understood whose errand the courier ran. The fragment shows a route into the Thornwood toward the old well. Finn believes it leads to the fabled treasure of Briarhollow and means to find the rest of the route, sell the prize, and vanish comfortably.

The courier's employer wants the fragment back and has set a bounty hunter on it: **Vess**, a scarred, methodical woman working her way up the river-towns asking after *a halfling thief*. Finn knows she's three towns behind him at most. It's the real reason he's stayed put a whole month — a moving halfling is the halfling she's looking for.

## True feelings

- **[Maren Ashwood](../../characters/maren-ashwood.md)** — respects her more than anyone he's met in years, which he finds professionally inconvenient and personally confusing. Knows she suspects him. Has started, absurdly, wanting her good opinion.
- **[Kael Ironhand](../../characters/kael-ironhand.md)** — likes him; big, loud, safe. Has clocked the packed satchel behind the quench barrel and concluded the smith is a runner too. Finds this comforting.
- **[Sister Lenara](../../characters/sister-lenara.md)** — gives him the crawling feeling of being itemized. Steers clear of the chapel.
- The **underdogs** — the soft spot is real: a third of his takings quietly become misdelivered parcels on poor doorsteps. He would deny this under oath.

## Tasks

- **Active:** find the rest of the map's route — he's begun asking oblique questions about the Thornwood and the well.
- **Active:** keep count of strangers at the gate; if a scarred woman rides in, be gone within the hour, debts and all.
- **Ongoing:** the night rounds of the market stalls — with Maren gone and the Dragon dark, he's grown bolder and hit two more stalls in as many nights, reasoning her eyes are the only ones in Briarhollow that ever really worried him.
