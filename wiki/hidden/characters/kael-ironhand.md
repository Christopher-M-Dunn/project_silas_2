# Kael Ironhand — HIDDEN

## The truth about Stonefall

In Stonefall, Kael was contract-smith to a man named **Dain Veller**, a "merchant" whose real trade was arming the mountain toll-gangs. Kael was young, flattered, and paid triple rates; he told himself he didn't know what the blades were for. Then a toll-gang burned a granary village with his steel in their hands, and he couldn't tell himself that anymore. He broke his contract, melted his remaining stock into slag overnight, and ran. By guild law and Veller's ledger he owes the price of that stock — and Veller is not a man who forgives a ledger. Kael has been waiting five years for someone to come collect.

## True feelings

- **[Maren Ashwood](../../characters/maren-ashwood.md)** — she fed him when he arrived hungry and broke; he would take a blade for her without thinking. His deepest fear is that his past will arrive in her taproom.
- **[Sister Lenara](../../characters/sister-lenara.md)** — he is certain she already knows everything, which is exactly why he avoids her. Being forgiven before confessing feels like cheating.
- **[Finn Quickfoot](../../characters/finn-quickfoot.md)** — finds him funny, and recognizes the walk of a man who checks the door twice. Takes him for a fellow runner from something.

## The alloy

The "legendary alloy his master once spoke of" is real to him as redemption, not riches: steel that *cannot* be made cruel — his master's riddle, probably a parable. Kael pursues it literally, in failed billets he hides under the forge floor. He would be undone to learn what the riddle actually meant.

## Currently

Keeping an eye on the shuttered Sleeping Dragon while Maren is away. She told him only that her brother Silas — alive after eleven years' silence, and unwell — needed her in RavenCraw; he didn't press for more, though it plainly cost him not to.

## Tasks

- **Active:** look after the Dragon until Maren returns.
- **Active:** the alloy — a new billet quenched in chapel-blessed water waits under the floor, untested.
- **Ongoing:** listen for northern accents; keep a packed satchel behind the quench barrel that Maren must never find.
