# Juno Park — HIDDEN

*Fate after the evacuation. Draft, subject to retcon until narrated.*

Juno's pod came down hard and far, skipping off the ocean like a thrown stone before settling holed and slowly taking water. She got out with a tool sling, a bad gash over one eyebrow, and her opinion of pod manufacturing intact, and was treading water among the debris when the water around her began to glow — a [Sorrai](../world/sorrai.md) kin-raft, drawn through the dark to the point of terror they'd felt cut across the sky.

She is now the involuntary guest and absolute fascination of the Sorrai: the first mind they have ever held that can *feel one thing and do another*. They keep her warm, fed on things she is trying not to think about, and constantly, disconcertingly understood. Juno — who ran on the principle that nobody gets to know what she's feeling — is coping about as well as expected, which the Sorrai, of course, also know.

**Day 1, through first dark:** hours in the water before the glow found her — the kin-raft homed on her fear across miles and hauled her out as the light failed. She spent first dark aboard: gash cleaned and sealed with something warm and alive she has chosen not to examine, fed, surrounded by sleek shapes that answer her sarcasm with the *feeling* of sarcasm, perfectly returned. Her pod settled lower before she could strip it; she memorized the spot by the debris field. She has not slept. They know she has not slept, and have projected, gently, the suggestion of sleep at her four times. She's counting.

**Day 2, dawn through second dusk:** wore the kin-raft down instead of the reverse — badgered them, in the only language that works on them, into ferrying her back to the debris field at first light before her pod finished sinking. Got aboard it one last time waist-deep, tool sling and all, and stripped what she could reach: a signal fragment, a chunk of the nav board, cable she didn't have a use for yet but grabbed anyway. The pod went under for good twenty minutes after she left it. That done, six on the water together — she counted — and no fear left to project sleep at, she finally slept, hard, most of the daylight hours, curled in the lee of two of them with a third keeping something like watch. Woke at second dusk annoyed at herself for how good it felt to be tended.

**Second dark, to Day 3's dawn:** woke up enough of her old self to start taking the salvage apart by feel in the dark, muttering at it the way she mutters at everything. No transmission yet — the fragment's promising, the nav board scrap is mostly dead weight — but she's got a working theory of which parts are worth keeping, and the Sorrai, fascinated as ever, have taken to handing her pieces of debris unprompted whenever her attention snags on something. Watched dawn come up without meaning to pull an all-nighter, and did anyway.

**Through Day 3's daylight and dark, to Day 4's dawn:** kept at the salvage in stolen hours, cranky about the lack of proper tools and increasingly convinced the fragment can be coaxed into a short-range transmission if she can rig a power source that isn't "whatever the Sorrai are willing to let her improvise with organic material." No breakthrough yet, but she's stopped calling it hopeless out loud, which the Sorrai, of course, noticed immediately and were insufferably pleased about. Slept in more reasonable stretches this time, no longer fighting it as hard.

**Second and third hours of light:** back at the salvage, muttering, the Sorrai still handing her debris on request like an over-eager lab assistant. No breakthrough on the power source yet, but she's narrowed the problem down out loud enough times that even the Sorrai seem to be following the shape of it now, which she finds almost more annoying than not being understood at all.

## Tasks

- **Active:** stay alive; learn the rules of wherever she is.
- **Done:** salvage anything electronic from her pod before it sinks entirely.
- **Active:** get the fragment transmitting — power source is the remaining blocker.
- **Ongoing:** not feel things. (Failing, less than before.)

**Synced:** Day 4 since planetfall, roughly third hour of light
