# Escape Pod — HIDDEN

*What the occupant doesn't know yet. Draft, subject to retcon until narrated.*

The pod is afloat on open water, sound and stable — and it has been noticed. A [[ktar|Ktar]] of middling age and inquisitive temperament has been closing on it, unhurried, since it splashed down; by the pod's viewport it will read as a shoreline that is somehow nearer each time one looks. The Ktar means no harm. It means to *collect* — small drifting things get gathered onto the back, considered, and grown around. When the pod grounds on living green, the conversation will have begun.

The [[sorrai|Sorrai]] felt this pod's descent too; a kin-raft is a day or two of travel away and arguing, in the wordless way of the Sorrai, about whether the terror they felt was something needing rescue.
