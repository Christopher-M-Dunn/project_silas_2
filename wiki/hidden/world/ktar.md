# The Ktar — HIDDEN

The oldest people of [Ktarluud](ktarluud.md) — and its geography. Every floating island on the world-ocean is the living back of a Ktar: a being the size of a meadow or a county, part animal and part orchard, its topside grown into the tangled paradise the crew glimpsed from orbit — green and copper and violet, fruiting freely, flexing with the swell. They drift where they choose, gather into archipelago-moots, and disperse; the world is named for them ("the gathered backs of the Ktar").

**Their unique attribute: they speak through their gardens.** A Ktar has no face and no voice. It expresses itself in what it grows, ripens, opens, and withholds — a language of fruit and bloom and path and shelter that the other peoples read fluently and that even a stranger feels before understanding: a welcoming Ktar *provides*, precisely and personally; a displeased one goes barren underfoot. To be carried by a Ktar that likes you is to live in a garden that keeps quietly setting the table. They think in seasons, remember in centuries, and are very hard to alarm and very slow to forgive.

Anything that lands on a Ktar has, whether it knows it or not, begun a conversation.

**Disposition toward the newcomers:** to be discovered — one raft at a time.
