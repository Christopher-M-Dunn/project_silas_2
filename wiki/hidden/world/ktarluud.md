# Ktarluud — HIDDEN

*The world's own truth. Unknown to the crew; the player's character does not know the planet's name.*

The world is called **Ktarluud** in the trade-speech its peoples share — an old contraction of *Ktar-luud*, "the gathered backs of the Ktar," because from any high place the world is exactly that: a warm freshwater ocean, pole to pole, carrying the drifting living islands that are the [[ktar|Ktar]] themselves. The name honors the oldest species; the other two keep it without argument.

Three intelligent peoples share the world:

- **[[veth|The Veth]]** — few, brilliant, and builders. Theirs are the fixed lands: stony mountain-islands rooted to the seabed, which the other peoples visit but do not dwell on. Their technology is centuries ahead of the *Kestrel*'s in some directions and strangely indifferent in others.
- **[[sorrai|The Sorrai]]** — the feeling-people. They read and project every emotion, trust and malice included; nothing with a heart is a stranger to them for long.
- **[[ktar|The Ktar]]** — the islands. Vast, slow, and alive: every floating raft of green is the back of one.

Beneath them, a multitude of lesser life: bubble-groves that burst in warm rain, silver shoal-flocks that leap in ribbons, kite-grazers riding the amber sky, and stranger things in the deep water that even the Ktar do not name.

The crash of the *Kestrel* did not go unnoticed. All three peoples, in their different ways, know something fell out of the sky — the sky, which here never shows its stars.
