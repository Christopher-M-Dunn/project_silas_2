# SV *Kestrel*

A long-range survey vessel, crew of four, years past her refit date and held together by [[juno-park]]'s spite-based maintenance. Cramped, familiar, home: a two-deck spine of lab, galley, engineering, and a cockpit with one cracked seat nobody ever fixed because it was Captain [[roland-voss|Voss]]'s and he said it kept him humble.

Lost in an uncharted region of space — how, exactly, is a question for calmer hours — she came down somewhere on [[the-planet]]. The order to abandon ship came late and loud. Where the *Kestrel* herself struck, and what's left of her, is unknown.
