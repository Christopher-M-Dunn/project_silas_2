# RavenCraw

A small walled town at the far end of the north road, several days' hard travel from Briarhollow. Chimney-smoke on the horizon, a weathered sign, worn edges — little more of it has been seen: [[player|Christopher]] arrived spent on a fifth night of travel and has so far gotten no further than a hollow against a low stone wall outside the town's edge.

By Christopher's own account he keeps a laboratory here, and a message left at the front desk of the **Slippery Gypsy Inn** will find its way to him.

**Connections:** the north road, running several days south to Briarhollow's [[briarhollow-town-square|town]].
