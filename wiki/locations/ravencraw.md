# RavenCraw

A small walled town at the far end of the north road, several days' hard travel from Briarhollow — cramped where Briarhollow is open, grown up entirely inside its wall with no room to spread. Narrow cobbled lanes, upper floors leaning out until they nearly touch overhead, a wharf or caravan yard somewhere off in the dark where chains creak against something heavy at night. A gate guard keeps the night watch and knows [[player|Christopher]] on sight — evidently a known local, not just a passing traveler.

Home to the [[slippery-gypsy-inn|Slippery Gypsy Inn]], where Silas Ashwood lodges, and [[christophers-laboratory|Christopher's laboratory]], a cluttered set of rooms above a shuttered apothecary's.

**Connections:** the north road, running several days south to Briarhollow's [[briarhollow-town-square|town]].
