# The Raft ("Rusty")

*Revealed: the player tested it directly — three deliberate thumps against the matting — and it thumped back, off-beat and unmistakably intentional. Whatever this island is, it is aware, and it is listening. The player has taken to privately calling it Rusty, for its copper-toned growth.*

A vast mat of floating vegetation, one of the drifting islands glimpsed from the [[escape-pod|pod]]'s viewport at splashdown — thick, fibrous growth in green, copper, and violet, dense enough underfoot to bear weight, moving with the swell but never breaking apart, its far side lost in haze. It is not alone out here: at least three more raft-shapes are visible from its tallest hummocks, along with a fixed grey landmass, clearly not water-colored, glimpsed first during the crash-descent.

Fruit-like growths hang low near the waterline; a handheld spectrograph reads them (and other local growth) as full of sugars and organic compounds, no heavy metals, but otherwise unmatched against any shipboard database — not flagged safe, not flagged unsafe. The player ate some anyway: no adverse reaction, only relief, and watched the eaten cluster visibly regrow within minutes. No animal life lives here at all — no droppings, no burrows, nothing with teeth — only a walking path that stays uncannily, consistently convenient.

**It is confirmed aware and communicative.** Knocked on directly, it knocks back — mirrors novel rhythms exactly, and once reproduced a full three-minute song from a single hearing before adding an original phrase of its own, shaped like a question. It offers what's needed before being fully asked: fruit that grows back the moment it's wanted, cordage that toughens in the hand mid-pull. It can also move itself with intent, not just drift — it closed distance to the recovered [[escape-pod]] unprompted and rose around the hull to cradle it in place.

Asked — by orientation, pressure, and a repeated pointing gesture — to travel toward the grey landmass, it turned and is now doing so, steadily, through the first night since the crash.

**Where:** open water somewhere on [[the-planet]], underway toward the fixed grey landmass, [[escape-pod|the recovered pod]] held against its edge.

**Currently:** the player's first solid ground since the crash, first confirmed contact with something aware on this world, and now their transport — carrying them through the night toward a coastline neither has seen up close.
