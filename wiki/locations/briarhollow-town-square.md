# Briarhollow Town Square

A cobblestone square surrounded by timber-framed shops, with a weathered stone fountain at its center. Market stalls line the edges on busy days — and lately their owners grumble about goods going missing overnight.

**Connections:** the [The Sleeping Dragon Tavern](sleeping-dragon-tavern.md) is a minute away; the [The Blacksmith's Forge](blacksmiths-forge.md) a minute east; the [The Old Chapel](old-chapel.md) a minute north; the [Briarhollow East Gate](briarhollow-east-gate.md) two minutes southeast.
