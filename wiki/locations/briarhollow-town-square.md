# Briarhollow Town Square

A cobblestone square surrounded by timber-framed shops, with a weathered stone fountain at its center. Market stalls line the edges on busy days — and lately their owners grumble about goods going missing overnight. The thefts have gotten bolder this week, two more stalls hit since the [[sleeping-dragon-tavern]] closed its doors.

**Connections:** the [[sleeping-dragon-tavern]] is a minute away; the [[blacksmiths-forge]] a minute east; the [[old-chapel]] a minute north; the [[briarhollow-east-gate]] two minutes southeast.
