# The Blacksmith's Forge

An open-air forge where the rhythmic clang of hammer on anvil echoes across the eastern square. Sparks fly as molten metal meets cold water; tools and weapons line the walls, among them a well-balanced [Iron Shortsword](../items/iron-shortsword.md) waiting for a buyer. It is hot here, and unwary fingers get burned.

**Where:** a minute east of the [Briarhollow Town Square](briarhollow-town-square.md).

**Usually here:** [Kael Ironhand](../characters/kael-ironhand.md), dawn to dusk, singing badly over the anvil.
