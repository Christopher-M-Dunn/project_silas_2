# The Blacksmith's Forge

An open-air forge where the rhythmic clang of hammer on anvil echoes across the eastern square. Sparks fly as molten metal meets cold water; tools and weapons line the walls, among them a well-balanced [[iron-shortsword]] waiting for a buyer. It is hot here, and unwary fingers get burned.

**Where:** a minute east of the [[briarhollow-town-square]].

**Usually here:** [[kael-ironhand]], dawn to dusk, singing badly over the anvil.
