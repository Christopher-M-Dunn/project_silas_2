# Thornwood Forest Edge

Tall, ancient oaks crowd together, their branches forming a thick canopy overhead. The air is cool and damp, filled with bird calls and the crunch of fallen leaves. The town feels far away the moment the canopy closes. Townsfolk go in pairs and come out before dark.

**Connections:** five minutes west back to the [[briarhollow-east-gate]]; ten minutes deeper to the [[thornwood-clearing]].
