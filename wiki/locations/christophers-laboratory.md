# Christopher's Laboratory

A cramped, cluttered set of rooms above a shuttered apothecary's in [[ravencraw|RavenCraw]] — [[player|Christopher]]'s own workspace, thick with the particular disorder of a place nobody else is allowed to tidy. First actually seen on the 29th of Greenveil, when he returned there to recover and to reach out to **the elders**.

The elders themselves remain unmet and undescribed — a council Christopher answers to, reached by some kind of projected call. Their first response gave a full week before they could assemble; contacted again once that week had passed, their second answer came shorter and colder, and ended with word that they'd receive Christopher soon, on their own timing rather than his.

**Connections:** in RavenCraw, a short walk from the [[slippery-gypsy-inn|Slippery Gypsy Inn]].
