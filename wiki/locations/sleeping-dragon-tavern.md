# The Sleeping Dragon Tavern

A warm, dimly lit tavern with rough-hewn wooden beams and the smell of roasting meat. A fire crackles in a stone hearth; mugs clink and low conversations fill the air. [Maren Ashwood](../characters/maren-ashwood.md)'s venison stew is the pride of the house.

**Where:** Briarhollow. Stepping outside puts you in the [Briarhollow Town Square](briarhollow-town-square.md), a minute away. A narrow stair behind the bar switches back twice to a low, dusty loft under the eaves, used for storage and rarely visited.

**Usually here:** [Maren Ashwood](../characters/maren-ashwood.md) (keeper — she lives above the taproom), [Finn Quickfoot](../characters/finn-quickfoot.md) (lodger, most evenings holding court at a corner table), and whoever in Briarhollow wants a drink.

**Currently:** emptied and barred. Maren cleared the taproom in a hurry after [the violent struggle](../events/binding-of-the-green-book.md) in the loft — she believes the tavern and everyone in it are in danger. The hearth fire was left burning, unbanked, in the rush.
