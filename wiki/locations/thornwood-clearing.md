# Thornwood Clearing

A small clearing where sunlight breaks through the canopy. Wildflowers grow between mossy stones. At its center sits an old stone well, its rope frayed with age; no one in Briarhollow remembers who dug it, or why a well was wanted so far from any house.

**Connections:** ten minutes back toward the [Thornwood Forest Edge](thornwood-forest-edge.md).
