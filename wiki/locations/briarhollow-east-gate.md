# Briarhollow East Gate

A sturdy wooden gate flanked by two guard towers — usually manned by whichever townsman drew the short straw. Beyond it, a dirt road winds east into the Thornwood.

**Connections:** two minutes northwest to the [Briarhollow Town Square](briarhollow-town-square.md); five minutes east along the road to the [Thornwood Forest Edge](thornwood-forest-edge.md).
