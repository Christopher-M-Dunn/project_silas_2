# Escape Pod

One of the [*Kestrel*](the-kestrel.md)'s two single-berth escape pods: a padded coffin's worth of space, a viewport, a sensor readout, and survival stores meant for days, not months. It carried the [second-in-command](../characters/player.md) down alone.

It is now at rest — down and intact enough that its occupant is alive to wonder where "down" is. Through the viewport: amber light, moving water, and green. The sensor panel's steady verdict on the world outside: air breathable, temperature warm, life signs everywhere.

The nav display is cracked and dark — it won't confirm whether the beacon is reaching anyone, or whether anyone is reaching back. The transceiver itself still transmits fine: an automatic distress pulse now runs continuously, and repeated open-channel calls have gone unanswered. A hull-side water sampler reads the surrounding sea as dense with organic signal but unmatched against any hazard the ship's database knows — neither safe nor unsafe, just unrecognized. Averaged accelerometer readings put local gravity at roughly nine-tenths of a standard gravity.

No flotation gear was found aboard — the stores (rations, med kit, thermal sheet, tool roll) were built for a hull, not a swimmer. After drifting empty for a time, the pod was roped and hauled back to [The Raft ("Rusty")](the-raft.md), which closed most of the distance itself and then rose gently around the hull to cradle it in place rather than let it knock against the bank.

Back aboard, repeated radio calls still draw no voice response, but the cracked nav display briefly came alive: an intermittent, weak directional bearing on an unidentified signal, holding roughly the heading of the fixed grey landmass seen from the raft.

On Day 4, its cracked nav display was swapped for a working screen salvaged from the *Kestrel*'s wreck — full resolution restored. A clean spectrum sweep afterward turned up only [Voss](../characters/roland-voss.md)'s beacon; no sign yet of Juno or Sefa transmitting.

**Where:** nested against [The Raft ("Rusty")](the-raft.md), held by its growth rather than freely adrift.
