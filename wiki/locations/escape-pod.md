# Escape Pod

One of the [[the-kestrel|*Kestrel*]]'s two single-berth escape pods: a padded coffin's worth of space, a viewport, a sensor readout, and survival stores meant for days, not months. It carried the [[player|second-in-command]] down alone.

It is now at rest — down and intact enough that its occupant is alive to wonder where "down" is. Through the viewport: amber light, moving water, and green. The sensor panel's steady verdict on the world outside: air breathable, temperature warm, life signs everywhere.

**Where:** somewhere on [[the-planet]]. Precisely where remains to be seen — literally.
