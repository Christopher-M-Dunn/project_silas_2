# The Old Chapel

A small stone chapel with stained glass windows depicting forgotten saints. Dust motes drift through colored light; it smells of incense and old wood. Those who come with wounds or troubles leave a little lighter — [[sister-lenara]] keeps a [[healing-herb-pouch|pouch of medicinal herbs]] and older remedies besides.

**Where:** a minute north of the [[briarhollow-town-square]].

**Usually here:** [[sister-lenara]], who has tended it longer than anyone alive can remember.
