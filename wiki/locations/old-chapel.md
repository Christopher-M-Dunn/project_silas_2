# The Old Chapel

A small stone chapel with stained glass windows depicting forgotten saints. Dust motes drift through colored light; it smells of incense and old wood. Those who come with wounds or troubles leave a little lighter — [Sister Lenara](../characters/sister-lenara.md) keeps a [pouch of medicinal herbs](../items/healing-herb-pouch.md) and older remedies besides.

**Where:** a minute north of the [Briarhollow Town Square](briarhollow-town-square.md).

**Usually here:** [Sister Lenara](../characters/sister-lenara.md), who has tended it longer than anyone alive can remember.
