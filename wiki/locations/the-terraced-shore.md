# The Terraced Shore

*Not yet named. The near edge of the fixed grey landmass, reached at dawn on Day 3.*

The base of the cliff [[the-raft|Rusty]] carried the player to, seen up close for the first time at dawn. Unmistakably worked, not weathered: shallow, even terraces wide enough to walk two abreast, stone with a grain that looks grown and encouraged rather than cut. Plants grow along the terrace edges in careful lines — too regular for wild growth, not neglected enough to be abandoned. Something tends this, and recently.

A doorway opens into the cliff near the shore — no door in it, just a tall absence in the rock, dark past the first few feet. No sound or motion from it, though it doesn't read as truly empty.

On the third terrace up, tucked into a made niche square-cut into the rock at exactly hand height, the player found a small worked object — dense and dark, warm to the touch despite the shade, etched with a single fine spiral — placed precisely where a searching hand would find it. Nobody visible put it there.

Calling out — for the crew by name, then for anyone — got no verbal answer, but returning to the doorway and calling into it directly got a real response: a single low resonant tone from somewhere deep inside, felt as much as heard, and a soft blue-white light that kindled in the dark and held steady, neither approaching nor retreating.

Past the doorway, the passage opens into a hollow the rock was persuaded rather than cut into. [[the-terrace-dweller|Its inhabitant]] stood revealed there at the edge of the light, at dawn on Day 3 — the first sapient stranger seen face to face on this world. The player retreated, frightened; the dweller let them go without following.

**Where:** the fixed grey landmass, reached from open water via [[the-raft|Rusty]].

**Currently:** the player has since left this stretch of shore entirely — inland to the *Kestrel*'s wreck, then back to [[the-raft|Rusty]] at a different point on the same coastline. [[the-terrace-dweller]] presumably remains inside, unvisited since.

From the shore on Day 4, a thin, steady column of smoke was spotted rising from higher, unexplored country further inland on the same landmass — past the terraces, past the last visible ridge. Unlike weather: doesn't billow or spread, holds to one fixed point. Nothing encountered so far — Rusty, the sleek creatures, or the terrace dweller — has shown any sign of using fire. Source unknown.
