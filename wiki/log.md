**14th of Greenveil, Year 512 of the Broken Crown — morning** `session:2026-08-22-2024.md`
The traveler wakes by the hearth of the Sleeping Dragon for the first time. Maren recalls they were seen wandering Briarhollow three nights running before finally settling — unmoving — by her fire; she's been tending the fire and the blanket ever since, and still doesn't have a name for them.

**14th of Greenveil, Year 512 of the Broken Crown — morning** `session:2026-08-22-2024.md`
The traveler gives his name — Christopher — and says he was sent by **Silas Ashwood**, Maren's younger brother, from **RavenCraw**, several days north. Maren hasn't seen or heard from Silas in the eleven years since their parents' burying; she'd made peace with believing him dead. She does not yet know why he sent a messenger instead of coming himself, or how Christopher knew her by sight.

**14th of Greenveil, Year 512 of the Broken Crown — morning** `session:2026-08-22-2024.md`
Christopher tells Maren that Silas is unwell and she should see him soon, and relays a request: a small green book, wrapped in clothing, that should be in a wooden chest among their late father's effects — unless it was already given to the poor. Maren confirms such a chest exists, untouched in the tavern's loft for nine years since their parents' burying. She hasn't gone up to check yet.

**14th of Greenveil, Year 512 of the Broken Crown — morning** `session:2026-08-22-2024.md`
Christopher names the trouble: Silas believes himself afflicted by something evil centered on the green book, sapping his strength and clouding his mind. Christopher declares himself trained in magic both light and dark, with a laboratory in RavenCraw, and warns Maren the danger is real. Wary but unwilling to step back, Maren ties her apron off and leads him toward the loft to see the chest for herself before the book goes anywhere.

**14th of Greenveil, Year 512 of the Broken Crown — morning** `session:2026-08-22-2024.md`
In the loft above the taproom, Maren opens her father's chest for the first time in nine years and finds a shirt-wrapped bundle of the right size and shape near the bottom. Christopher offers no explanation of himself on the walk up, which she notes but doesn't press. She asks him to be the one to take the bundle out.

**14th of Greenveil, Year 512 of the Broken Crown — morning** `session:2026-08-22-2024.md`
The binding of the green book: Christopher wards his hands, unwraps the small green book, and fights whatever lives in it to a standstill through violent, escalating chanting. Maren is thrown down and struck her head; the loft nearly comes apart. The struggle ends with the book contained in a stable bluish-purple glow, bound with a cord from Maren's own hair. Exhausted, Christopher still won't say how he knew her on sight. Maren demands to know if the containment is actually safe, and whether her brother will be all right.

---

**14th of Greenveil, Year 512 of the Broken Crown — morning** `session:2026-08-22-2024.md`
Christopher ties the book around his neck on Maren's cord and warns her the binding spell will have drawn wraiths from a hundred miles around — the Sleeping Dragon and everyone in it are no longer safe. He gives her Silas's location, the Slippery Gypsy Inn in RavenCraw, and a way to reach him again by message there. Maren empties the taproom in minutes, sending Finn and the rest of Briarhollow's early drinkers out into the morning, and stands at the door with the bar in her hand, asking Christopher whether he's coming with her.

---

**14th of Greenveil, Year 512 of the Broken Crown — morning** `session:2026-08-22-2024.md`
Christopher refuses to let Maren travel with him, shapeshifts into a wolf without warning, and bolts north out of Briarhollow. Shaken, Maren bars the tavern door behind him.

**14th–19th of Greenveil, Year 512 of the Broken Crown** `catchup`
Five days pass (`/ff`). Christopher runs the road north to [[ravencraw|RavenCraw]] mostly in wolf-shape, resting in short, wary stretches — twice something catches his scent and thinks better of it, and no wraith runs him down. He reaches RavenCraw's outskirts spent on the fifth night and goes still, wolf-shaped, in a hollow against a low stone wall outside the town's edge.
