# The Binding of the Green Book

**Attendees:** [[player|Christopher]], [[maren-ashwood]].

**Experience:** In the loft above the [[sleeping-dragon-tavern]], Christopher warded his hands and unwrapped the [[small-green-book|small green book]] from Maren's father's chest. The moment the wrapping came off, whatever lived in the book fought back — a red light rapidly fading and flaring in time with Christopher's chanting, an unseen force battering the room, his voice climbing to something inhuman before it was over. Maren was thrown to the floor and against the wall, struck her head on a crate, and had to shield her ears against the sound. The struggle ended with the book suspended inside a stable bluish-purple glow — contained, for now, though nobody in the room has said the danger has passed. Christopher, exhausted, asked Maren for a ribbon; she gave up the cord from her own braid.

**Unresolved:** whether the book is truly safe to move, what exactly Christopher did, and what it means for [[silas-ashwood|Silas]], still afflicted in RavenCraw.
