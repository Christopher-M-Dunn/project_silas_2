# Maren's Journey to RavenCraw

**Revealed:** Maren walks into Silas's room at the Slippery Gypsy Inn, travel-worn and clearly having come a very long way, just as Christopher's week-long time-suspension ends.

**Experience:** Maren made the length of the north road from Briarhollow to RavenCraw on foot and alone, arriving road-worn in a way that speaks to how hard the trip was for a woman who hadn't traveled past the market in a decade. The particulars of the road — when she left, who she told, what she saw — are hers to tell, and haven't come up yet.
