# Captain Roland Voss

Captain of the [[the-kestrel|*Kestrel*]] and its crew of four. A career surveyor in his fifties, grey-cropped and broad, with the patience of a man who has been wrong exactly often enough to distrust being sure. By-the-book to a degree that maddens his officers and has kept every one of them alive — until, perhaps, now. His dry, buried humor surfaces about once a week, always when it's least expected and most needed.

To his second-in-command he has been mentor, sparring partner, and occasionally a wall: he asks for counterarguments and then argues with them. He does not say what he thinks of people; he assigns them things, and the things say it.

**Fate:** unknown. Last seen in the chaos of the evacuation.
