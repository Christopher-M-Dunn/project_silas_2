# Juno Park

Ship's engineer of the [[the-kestrel|*Kestrel*]]. Small, quick, permanently smudged; talks to machinery in a low conversational mutter and to people in short bursts, as if human interaction were an interruption to a more important dialogue. Has kept the aging *Kestrel* flying years past its refit date through a mixture of genius, scavenged parts, and what she calls "spite-based maintenance." Fiercely loyal in deeds and allergic to saying so.

She and the second-in-command have a standing arrangement: they don't report each other's unauthorized fixes.

**Fate:** unknown. Last seen in the chaos of the evacuation.
