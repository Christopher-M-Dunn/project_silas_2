# Kael Ironhand

The town blacksmith, at the [The Blacksmith's Forge](../locations/blacksmiths-forge.md). A broad-shouldered human man in his thirties with a shaved head, thick forearms covered in old burn scars, and a warm smile that contrasts with his imposing frame; a leather apron hangs over his chest.

Kael is boisterous, generous, and fiercely loyal — the sort who laughs from the belly and would go hungry to feed a friend. He treats each piece of metalwork as art and talks freely about the one dream of his craft: perfecting a legendary alloy his old master once spoke of. He is honest to a fault and famously terrible at keeping secrets — which makes it all the more noticeable that about one subject he keeps one: he came to Briarhollow five years ago from the northern city of Stonefall, and he does not talk about why he left. It clearly weighs on him.

**Usually found:** at his anvil, dawn to dusk.

**As seen about town:** devoted to [Maren Ashwood](maren-ashwood.md), his first friend here — everyone knows she fed him when he arrived hungry and broke. Around [Sister Lenara](sister-lenara.md) he is respectful and faintly nervous, like a man who suspects he's been read.
