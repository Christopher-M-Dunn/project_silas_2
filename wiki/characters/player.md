# Christopher (player)

*This page is the player's character. It has no hidden side — the player's mind belongs to the player.*

A traveler lately arrived in Briarhollow, going by **Christopher**. Seen wandering the town three nights running, he finally stopped moving by the hearth of the [[sleeping-dragon-tavern]] and fell into the deep, strange sleep of [[rules|torpor]] — until now. Face and history: still not established.

He greeted [[maren-ashwood]] by name on waking, though she never gave it — a small mystery neither of them has explained yet. He says he was sent to Briarhollow by [[silas-ashwood|Silas Ashwood]], Maren's estranged brother, from a town called **RavenCraw** several days' journey north — that Silas is unwell and she should see him soon, and that Silas asked for a small green book, wrapped in clothing, that should be in a wooden chest among his effects, if it wasn't given to the poor. How Christopher can describe Maren well enough to know her on sight, he hasn't yet said.

He's told Maren he is trained in "all manner of magiks, both light and dark," and keeps a laboratory in RavenCraw. He believes Silas is afflicted by something powerful and evil, centered on the book, and wants to bring it back to RavenCraw to analyze — he's warned Maren he can't guarantee her safety if she involves herself further than necessary.

He's made good on the claim: warding his hands, unwrapping the [[small-green-book]], and fighting whatever lived in it to a standstill in a loud, violent exchange of chanted phrases — see [[binding-of-the-green-book]]. He can raise his voice to something inhuman when pressed. He offered no explanation of how he knew Maren's face and name when she asked again on the way to the loft.

He now wears the bound book around his neck on Maren's red hair-cord. He's warned her the binding spell was loud enough to draw wraiths from a hundred miles around, and that the [[sleeping-dragon-tavern]] and everyone in it are no longer safe — he's told her to evacuate and lock up immediately. He's given her a destination if she wants to find Silas herself: the **Slippery Gypsy Inn** in RavenCraw, with a way to leave him a message there.

He can shapeshift into a wolf — did so on the spot, refusing to let Maren travel with him, and bolted north out of Briarhollow in that form. He held the wolf-shape for most of five days' hard travel, reaching the outskirts of **RavenCraw** on the fifth night too spent to go further.

He can also cast a minor perception spell that keeps others from noticing he's underdressed or unclothed — not an illusion so much as a gap in what an onlooker's mind reports to itself, quietly filled in with something unremarkable. Cast it on himself re-entering RavenCraw after shifting back to human.

He's evidently a known resident of RavenCraw, not just a visitor — the night gate guard recognized him on sight and let him through without question, treating his ragged, unexplained state as unremarkable ("out late"). Innkeeper [[karl]] at the [[slippery-gypsy-inn|Slippery Gypsy Inn]] also knows him by name.

He can cast a major ward on himself — a pale blue glow that blocks outside magic from affecting him, but also blocks his own magic from working outward, including magic already running, like his minor perception spell. Learned this the costly way: cast the ward outside Silas's door without weighing that cost, silencing the perception spell and leaving himself glowing and visibly undressed in front of Karl (who noticed; Christopher didn't) and then Silas, without realizing it.

Assesses the book's entity as a magical familiar of unprecedented strength — female-presenting, by whatever sense tells him such things — and now suspects his binding of her may not have truly taken; that she could be feigning containment while she works out her next move. Plans to unbind the book to study her properly, and reached out to "the elders" — a council of his own, not yet described — for guidance, but they need a full week to assemble.

Unwilling to wait that long, he revealed a superpower to Silas, swearing him to secrecy: he can bend time around himself so he doesn't experience its passage, extend that to objects on him, and — at a near-total cost of his own aura — extend it to one other person. Used it on himself and Silas together to skip the week the elders needed, lying fully matched to Silas palm-to-palm and forehead-to-forehead to hold the effect around them both.

Gave Maren a partial explanation — that Silas is afflicted by something old and dangerous bound in their father's book, that he's trying to help, that he's more than he looks — without going into who or what he actually is. Contacted the elders again once their week was up; their answer came shorter and colder than before, ending with word they'll receive him soon, on their own timing.

**Condition:** in [[rules|torpor]], slumped over his own worktable at his laboratory in RavenCraw as of the 30th of Greenveil, the elders' summons still unanswered.

**Carrying:** the [[small-green-book]], bound in a stable glow and tied around his neck on a red cord.
