# Christopher (player)

*This page is the player's character. It has no hidden side — the player's mind belongs to the player.*

A traveler lately arrived in Briarhollow, going by **Christopher**. Seen wandering the town three nights running, he finally stopped moving by the hearth of the [[sleeping-dragon-tavern]] and fell into the deep, strange sleep of [[rules|torpor]] — until now. Face and history: still not established.

He greeted [[maren-ashwood]] by name on waking, though she never gave it — a small mystery neither of them has explained yet. He says he was sent to Briarhollow by **Silas Ashwood**, Maren's estranged brother, from a town called **RavenCraw** several days' journey north — that Silas is unwell and she should see him soon, and that Silas asked for a small green book, wrapped in clothing, that should be in a wooden chest among his effects, if it wasn't given to the poor. How Christopher can describe Maren well enough to know her on sight, he hasn't yet said.

He's told Maren he is trained in "all manner of magiks, both light and dark," and keeps a laboratory in RavenCraw. He believes Silas is afflicted by something powerful and evil, centered on the book, and wants to bring it back to RavenCraw to analyze — he's warned Maren he can't guarantee her safety if she involves herself further than necessary.

He's made good on the claim: warding his hands, unwrapping the [[small-green-book]], and fighting whatever lived in it to a standstill in a loud, violent exchange of chanted phrases — see [[binding-of-the-green-book]]. He can raise his voice to something inhuman when pressed. He offered no explanation of how he knew Maren's face and name when she asked again on the way to the loft.

He now wears the bound book around his neck on Maren's red hair-cord. He's warned her the binding spell was loud enough to draw wraiths from a hundred miles around, and that the [[sleeping-dragon-tavern]] and everyone in it are no longer safe — he's told her to evacuate and lock up immediately. He's given her a destination if she wants to find Silas herself: the **Slippery Gypsy Inn** in RavenCraw, with a way to leave him a message there.

He can shapeshift into a wolf — did so on the spot, refusing to let Maren travel with him, and bolted north out of Briarhollow in that form. He held the wolf-shape for most of five days' hard travel, reaching the outskirts of **RavenCraw** on the fifth night too spent to go further.

**Condition:** exhausted, in wolf form, asleep against a stone wall outside RavenCraw.

**Carrying:** the [[small-green-book]], bound in a stable glow and tied around his neck on a red cord.
