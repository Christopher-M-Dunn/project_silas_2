# The Terrace Dweller

*Not yet named. First seen Day 3, dawn, inside the doorway on [[the-terraced-shore]].*

Tall and slight, long-limbed in a way that reads as elegant rather than awkward. Dark, faintly sheened skin like polished slate. No visible hair. Large black eyes, too big for the narrow face, clearly built for the dark the doorway's passage sits in.

Left the [[spiral-token]] on the terrace before showing itself, then answered a called-out greeting from just inside the doorway with a low resonant tone and a kindled blue-white light — an invitation, not an approach. When the player came in, it held its ground at the edge of the light: didn't retreat, didn't close the distance, both hands open and empty at its sides. Hasn't spoken, if it speaks at all.

When the player retreated, frightened, it didn't follow — tracked them to the doorway with just its eyes, a slight tilt of the head its only visible reaction, and let them go without a sound.

**Currently:** presumably still inside, past the doorway. Hasn't been seen since the player left.
