# Dr. Sefa Naim

Medic and survey scientist of the [[the-kestrel|*Kestrel*]] — the reason a crew of four needed a xenobiology lab. Gentle, unhurried, endlessly curious; the sort of person who treats a scraped knuckle and a first-contact protocol with the same calm attention. Keeps a private field journal in longhand, sketches in the margins. Of everyone aboard, Sefa is the one who would have wanted to crash on a living world, if crashing were the price.

They have patched up the second-in-command more than once, and have a way of asking "and how are you otherwise?" that makes lying feel like poor sportsmanship.

**Fate:** unknown. Last seen in the chaos of the evacuation.
