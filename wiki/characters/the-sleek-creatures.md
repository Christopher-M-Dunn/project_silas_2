# The Sleek Creatures

*Not yet named by the player. First encountered at dawn, Day 3, in the shallows below the cliff.*

Five of them, sighted first as a wrong shimmer on the water the night before, then unmistakably as coordinated shapes closing distance at dawn. Amphibious, sleek-hided, paler at the throat, whiskered, built somewhere between an otter and something larger — round heads that turn to track a person the way a person's does, wide dark eyes with none of an animal's flatness. They swim with an ease that makes a human stroke look like drowning.

They left [Rusty](../locations/the-raft.md) alone and converged directly on the second-in-command the moment they went into open water alone. They do not close the last few feet uninvited — they hold position, watching, until approached.

**They project feeling.** Closing distance in the shallows, the nearest one sent something that was unmistakably not the second-in-command's own: a wash of deliberate calm, landing over real fear like a hand placed flat on it. Not sound, not scent — arriving from outside, and unmistakably external. What they are, how many kinds of feeling they can send, and whether they can also read one back: unknown so far, but the calm didn't feel random.

They don't appear to parse spoken words or symbolic gesture as such — miming a falling pod and pointing to the horizon got no sign of literal comprehension. But asked, however clumsily, about "the others," the calm shifted for a moment into something older and heavier: a brief, secondhand echo of fear, distinctly **four-fold**. Whatever they understood of the question, something in it touched a memory they already had — of more than one person falling from the sky.

When the second-in-command left to head inland, they didn't follow past the waterline — held position in the shallows, watching, and sent one last small feeling after them: quicker and lighter than the earlier calm, closer to curiosity, gone almost as soon as it landed.

Reappeared at [Rusty](../locations/the-raft.md)'s side on Day 4 as the second-in-command returned along the coast with a second human — Captain Voss — in tow. Rose in the shallows to read this new mind the same way they read the first, open and patient, ignoring Kristoff entirely in favor of the stranger.

**Currently:** fanned out in the shallows below Rusty, five of them again, all attention fixed on Voss.
