# Sister Lenara

Tends the [[old-chapel]]. A tall, slender elven woman with pale silvery skin, long white hair, and luminous green eyes; she wears simple grey robes and moves with an ethereal grace.

Lenara has kept the chapel for over two centuries — she watched the kingdom rise and fall, and speaks of that past only in riddles, when she speaks of it at all. She is gentle, contemplative, and unsettlingly empathetic: she often knows what a visitor needs before they ask, and her soft words carry weight. She mends wounds with prayer, herb-craft, and — the town whispers — something older than either. She believes all things must find their balance.

**Usually found:** in the chapel, at prayer or tending someone.

**As seen about town:** kind to everyone and close to no one. She has quietly offered [[kael-ironhand]] her counsel more than once; he deflects, and she does not press. She takes a patient interest in guiding Briarhollow's young toward wisdom.
