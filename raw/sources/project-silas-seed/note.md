# Source: project_silas starter seed

Ingested 2026-08-22 (workshop), copied from `../project_silas` (the original codebase this project replaces): `data/seed/starter_world.yaml`, `data/seed/character_templates.yaml`, `config/world_defaults.yaml`.

What was taken: the world of Aldenmere — Briarhollow and the Thornwood, seven locations and their connections, four townsfolk with their traits, goals, relationships, and backstories, and three starter items.

What was deliberately dropped, per the prose-first design: all numeric mechanics (trait intensities, -10..+10 relationship scores, healing/memory/relationship tuning constants, per-species body-part templates). Numbers were translated into prose stances on the character pages.

Elaborations: the seed left its secrets as open hooks (who steals from the market, what Kael fled, what lies beneath the chapel, where the map fragment leads). The hidden pages give these concrete, modest answers so the test world is playable; they knit the hooks into one connected thread and can be freely rewritten before real play.
