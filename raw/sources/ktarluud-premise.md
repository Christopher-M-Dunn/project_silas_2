# Source: Ktarluud world premise

Authored by the player in workshop, 2026-08-23. The premise, as specified:

> Set on a lush planet similar to Perelandra (C.S. Lewis). World: called **Ktarluud** to the locals, unknown to the crew. My crew (I was 2nd in command) was lost in an uncharted region of space. 14-hour day/night cycles; the air on the planet is safe to breathe; inhabited by 3 different intelligent species and a multitude of lesser flora and fauna. One of the intelligent species is highly technically advanced, to the point where they could eventually work out a seamless way to communicate with me. One of the other main species can read and project all emotions, including trust and malice. The third main species will have some other unique attribute. Use the same rules.md. My spaceship crashed there and had 3 other occupants. My current location is in an escape pod, by myself.

Elaboration decisions by the World-Keeper (all hidden-layer material remains draft, subject to retcon):

- Perelandra reference absorbed as: a warm freshwater ocean world under a thick, luminous amber sky (no visible sun or stars), floating vegetation rafts in constant motion, effortless natural abundance, and rare fixed land as the exception.
- The third species' unique attribute, the species' names, the ship, the three crewmates' identities, and everyone's post-crash fates were invented by the Keeper — crew identities on public pages (the player knew their own crew), everything undiscovered on hidden pages.
- The name "Ktarluud" is known to the player as author but not to their character; it lives on the hidden world page until learned in play.
